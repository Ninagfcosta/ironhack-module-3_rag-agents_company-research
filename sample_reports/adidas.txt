### Company Overview: Adidas

**Adidas AG** is a major global corporation specializing in athletic footwear, apparel, accessories, and equipment. Founded in 1949 by Adolf "Adi" Dassler in Herzogenaurach, Germany, Adidas has grown to become one of the world's leading sportswear manufacturers. As of 2023, it is recognized as the second-largest producer of sportswear globally after Nike.

- **Headquarters:** Herzogenaurach, Germany
- **Industry:** Athletic Footwear and Apparel
- **Revenue:** Approximately €23 billion for 2024
- **Logo:** The iconic three-stripe logo

Adidas is known for its strong brand presence in sports, having made significant contributions to various athletic events, including the FIFA World Cup. The company has a reputation for product innovation and partnerships with prominent athletes and celebrities.

#### Key Historical Points
- Adidas introduced its first item of apparel, the Franz Beckenbauer tracksuit, in 1967.
- The brand has faced both success and challenges, including fluctuating market shares and strategic partnerships with stars such as Lionel Messi and Kanye West.
- The company went public in 1989, marking its transformation into a publicly traded entity.

#### Additional Information
Adidas is also the largest manufacturer of sports bras in Europe and a significant supplier of team kits for international football clubs. The brand actively engages in collaborations, such as the Yeezy line with Kanye West.
