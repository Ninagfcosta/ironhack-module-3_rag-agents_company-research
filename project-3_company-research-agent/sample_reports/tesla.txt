Report for Tesla:
Here is a comprehensive overview of Tesla, Inc.:

### Company Overview
- **Name**: Tesla, Inc. (formerly Tesla Motors, Inc. until 2017)
- **Founded**: 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning.
- **Headquarters**: Austin, Texas, USA.
- **Industry**: Automobile and Energy Technology.
- **Website**: [www.tesla.com](https://www.tesla.com)

Tesla is a public company and a significant player in the electric vehicle (EV) market. The company is known for pioneering and popularizing electric cars, particularly through its high-performance models like the Tesla Roadster, Model S, Model 3, Model X, and Model Y. Tesla has also ventured into energy generation and storage solutions, harnessing technology to reduce reliance on fossil fuels.

### Products and Services
Tesla's business is divided primarily into two segments:
1. **Automotive**: Manufacturing and selling electric vehicles.
2. **Energy Generation and Storage**: Including solar panels, solar roofs, and battery energy storage systems.
